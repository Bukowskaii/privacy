Privacy Policy - The Lightwell: Endless Idle TD

Bukowskaii Creations LLC · Effective 10 November 2026 · Contact: tom@bukowskaii.com

Summary

The Lightwell: Endless Idle TD ("the game") has no accounts of its own, and your progress is saved on your device. Most data that leaves your device goes to Google: through Google Play when you sign in to Play Games or make a purchase, through Google AdMob for the optional rewarded videos, and through Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous crash and error reports, which you can turn off in Settings. If you are signed in to Play Games, your Weekly runs also go to our own server, the Lightwell service at lightwell.bukowskaii.com, which replays them to build the Weekly ranking (see Weekly ranking). You can remove yourself from it in Settings.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses Play Games Services to back up your save to your Google account, and to record your achievements. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy, and describes what the Play Games SDK collects at developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data at any time (see Deleting your data).

Weekly ranking

The Weekly ranking is our own board, run by Bukowskaii Creations LLC on the Lightwell service (lightwell.bukowskaii.com). It is not a Google Play Games leaderboard. The game sends the following only when you are signed in to Play Games, and only for the Weekly:

  • Your Play Games player id and display name. The game asks Play Games for a one-time sign-in code, and our server exchanges it with Google for your player id for this game and your Play Games display name. The ranking shows your Play Games name, or "Anonymous #" and a number if you choose Anonymous in Settings > Weekly ranking > Ranking name. There are no free-text names.
  • Your Weekly runs. For each run: the week, a random run id, the game version, the game rules version, the list of moves you made (which tower, where and when), and the best wave and run length the game claims. The server replays the moves to check the wave before it ranks it. It never receives your save, your Lumens, your purchases, your advertising ID or your location.
  • A Play Integrity verdict. Each upload carries a Google Play Integrity token, which our server sends to Google to learn whether the request came from the genuine game on a genuine device. The server keeps only the verdict labels Google returns (such as "recognized by Play" and "meets device integrity"), never device identifiers. A run that fails the check is kept off the public board.
  • Your IP address, which the server uses in memory to limit how often a device can call it. It is not stored. The game also asks the server for the current time, so the Weekly is timed fairly. That request sends nothing but your IP address, and it is not logged.

How long we keep it: the move lists of runs for 8 weeks; the board rows (rank, name and wave for each week) as the ranking's history; your player record (player id and name choice) until you remove yourself. Data travels over HTTPS, is stored only on our server, and is not sold or shared.

To remove yourself, open Settings > Weekly ranking > Remove me. This deletes your player record, your name and all your runs and board rows from the server at once, and the game stops sending new runs until you choose Join the ranking again. You can also email tom@bukowskaii.com. If you never sign in to Play Games, the only thing the game sends to the Lightwell service is the time request.

Purchases

Purchases go through Google Play Billing. The game receives only the purchase record Google sends (which product, and its order and purchase tokens) and keeps it on your device so that a purchase is never granted twice and can be restored. It never sees your payment details.

Rewarded videos

The game offers optional rewarded videos: one plays only when you tap to watch it for a boost, and nothing else in the game shows ads. The videos come from Google AdMob. To serve them and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's IP address (which can estimate your general location), your interactions with the ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information about the app and the SDK, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads). A Supporter Pack skips the videos, so no ad is requested at all.

Crash and error reports (optional)

When the game hits an error or crashes, it can send an anonymous report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics, so that we can find and fix the problem. A report holds the kind of error and its technical stack trace, short breadcrumbs of what the game was doing (such as "app resumed" or "a sheet opened"), the game version, whether it is a release build, your best wave and number of zones, your device model and Android version, and a random Firebase installation ID that Crashlytics creates to count affected devices. It never holds your save, your Lumens, your purchases, your name, your email or any other personal information. Firebase keeps these reports for 90 days under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy and security terms. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Send error reports (it is on by default); when it is off, nothing is collected or sent.

Problem reports and save export

Settings has a Report a problem button. It copies a text report (game version, device model and Android version, a summary of your save, and recent error messages) to your clipboard. Export save copies your save as text to your clipboard. In both cases nothing is sent: the text goes only where you choose to paste it.

Deleting your data

The game has no accounts of its own, so there is nothing to close. You can delete everything it keeps:

  • Your save and purchase records on the device: uninstall the game, or clear its storage (Android Settings > Apps > The Lightwell > Storage > Clear storage).
  • Your Weekly ranking entries on our server: Settings > Weekly ranking > Remove me deletes them at once, or email tom@bukowskaii.com.
  • Your Play Games cloud save, achievements and any old leaderboard scores: delete the game's data in the Play Games app (Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, or remove just this game), or manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads). Google keeps AdMob data under its own retention rules in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Anything else: email tom@bukowskaii.com and we will delete anything you sent us (such as a pasted problem report) within 30 days.
