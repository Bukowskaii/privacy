Privacy Policy — The Plane: Endless Idle TD

Effective 27 October 2026 · Contact: tom@bukowskaii.com

Summary

The Plane: Endless Idle TD ("the game") collects no personal data of its own. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved on your device. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google, through Google Play, when you sign in to Play Games or make a purchase.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses Play Games Services to back up your save to your Google account and to record your achievements. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy, and describes what the Play Games SDK collects at developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data from your Google account settings.

Purchases

Purchases go through Google Play Billing. The game receives only the purchase record Google sends (which product, and its order and purchase tokens) and keeps it on your device so that a purchase is never granted twice and can be restored. It never sees your payment details.

Rewarded videos

The game offers optional rewarded videos: one plays only when you tap to watch it for a boost, and nothing else in the game shows ads. No ad network is built in yet, so no advertising data is collected. Before one is added, this section will be updated to name the provider and describe what it collects.

Problem reports and save export

Settings has a Report a problem button. It copies a text report (game version, device model and Android version, a summary of your save, and recent error messages) to your clipboard. Export save copies your save as text to your clipboard. In both cases nothing is sent: the text goes only where you choose to paste it.
